Transfer guide

Money transfer guide

Practical steps for common services. Availability, fees, exchange rates, and delivery times depend on your route; check the provider’s live quote.

MoMo

MTN Mobile Money

Use the official MTN channels and verify the recipient details before confirming.

Before sending

  1. Open the official Mobile Money menu or app.
  2. Enter the recipient number carefully.
  3. Confirm the displayed recipient name and fee.
  4. Never share your PIN or one-time code.

Important

Check the current fees and limits with MTN before transferring.

Airtel

Airtel Money

Use official Airtel Money channels and check the transaction details.

Before sending

  1. Open the official Airtel Money menu or app.
  2. Enter the recipient number carefully.
  3. Review the recipient name and displayed charge.
  4. Keep your PIN and verification codes private.

Important

Check the current fees and limits with Airtel before transferring.

BANK

Bank transfers

Confirm account details using a trusted contact channel.

Before sending

  1. Use your bank’s official app or branch.
  2. Double-check the beneficiary name and account.
  3. Review fees and transfer timing.
  4. Keep receipts and never disclose passwords.

Important

Contact your bank through its official website or number if unsure.

UMUNARA does not set transfer prices. Always check the current fee, exchange rate, payout options, and estimated delivery time in the provider’s official app or website before sending.
