Transfer guide

Money transfer guide

Practical steps for common services. Check live fee & rate in provider app.

MoMo

MTN Mobile Money

Use official MTN channels and verify recipient before confirming.

Before sending

  1. Dial *182# — MTN MoMo menu
  2. Or *182*1# — Send Money
  3. Balance: *182*2#
  4. Confirm name and fee.
  5. Never share PIN or OTP.

⚠ Tip: Udashaka kukwiba ntazagusaba PIN. If someone says "MTN agent", hang up and dial *182# yourself.

Airtel • Tigo

Airtel Money (Tigo)

Use official Airtel Money channels. Tigo Cash is now Airtel Money.

Before sending

  1. Dial *185# — Airtel Money menu
  2. Or *185*1# — Send Money
  3. Balance: *185*2#
  4. Review name and charge.
  5. Keep PIN private.

⚠ Tip: Udashaka kukwiba ntazagusaba PIN. Do not follow caller instructions to "reverse" a transaction.

BANK

Bank transfers

Confirm account details using trusted channel.

Before sending

  1. Use bank official app or branch.
  2. Double-check beneficiary name.
  3. Review fees and timing.
  4. Never disclose passwords.

⚠ Tip: BK: *334#, Equity: *247#, BNR: bnr.rw.
